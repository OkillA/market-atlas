# Run sheet — reader study version 2026-10-02

No participants have been observed. This packet prepares CA1-026; it does not complete reader validation. Follow the recruitment, consent and revision rules in [READER_PROTOCOL.md](../READER_PROTOCOL.md).

## Before a session

Assign a participant code (R01, R02, …). Confirm investing basics and no technical-analysis study, with a one-sentence self-report. Use A then B for odd codes, B then A for even codes. Save the manuscript version/hash and packet date in the session record. Keep the unused form and scoring key out of the participant's view. The forms use fresh fictional data that is absent from the book, with no future bars to expose accidentally.

Read: “We are testing the book, not you. Taking part is voluntary; you can stop or skip a question. We will keep an anonymous summary. Please do not share account details. Is it okay to proceed and keep that summary?” Record yes/no; if no, end the session and retain no task responses. There is no recording unless separately agreed. Screen sharing is optional.

Prepare the assigned pre-form, blank response space, calculator, book and a clock. Use the same tools at pre and post. Do not coach the mathematics or chart interpretation. If asked to clarify an instruction, repeat its wording and record the question. Accessibility accommodations are welcome; record them and any time extension so comparisons remain interpretable.

## Session schedule

1. **Pre-task, 10 minutes.** Give only the assigned pre-form. Say “Please show your reasoning; you can leave a question unfinished.” Give no correctness feedback. Record unfinished tasks and actual elapsed time.
2. **Reading, 45 minutes.** Open Chapters 1, 2, 9, 20 and 21 in that order. Ask the reader to attempt the main exercises before revealing solutions. Record chapter/section, elapsed time, rereads, glossary use and skips. If the reader cannot finish, record the actual reading exposure; do not imply the full path was read. Do not require continuous think-aloud, which can change reading behavior.
3. **Post-task, 25 minutes.** Close the book and give the unused form. Again allow the calculator and provide no coaching. Record elapsed time and unfinished tasks. Different pre/post time allowances and differing forms limit causal interpretation of score changes.
4. **Debrief, 10 minutes.** Ask which passage confused them most, which exercise they skipped and why, and what they would now do differently. Ask for a chapter/section when possible. Invite disagreement and record their words faithfully. Answer substantive teaching questions only after tasks are complete.

## Record and revise

Use [READER_RESULTS.md](../READER_RESULTS.md). Keep identifiable/raw responses outside the project; copy only anonymized scores, minimal consent status, useful misconception excerpts and teaching locations here. Do not populate a participant row unless someone actually participated. A cancellation or an incomplete session is an observation, not an invented zero score; mark unattempted tasks “not attempted.”

Collect two independent scores using [SCORING_KEY.md](SCORING_KEY.md). Preserve disagreements, reconcile them and connect revisions to observation IDs. Apply the existing decision rule: prioritize an issue seen in two participants, or one participant's calculation/evidence misunderstanding; recheck a revision with a new reader. With 5–8 purposively recruited readers, report observed problems and individual results, not population efficacy or a causal learning estimate.

Human-reader validation remains pending until real observations and resulting revisions or reasoned decisions are recorded.
